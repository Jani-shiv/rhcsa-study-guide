# Accessibility Statement

DevOps ni Diary is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to make learning Linux and DevOps as inclusive as possible.

---

## Conformance Status

The [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

The **RHCSA Study Guide** aims to conform to **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** standards.

---

## Accessibility Features Implemented

- **High Contrast & Dark Mode**: Full support for both high-contrast light and dark themes to accommodate visual preferences and sensitivities.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: All interactive elements (navigation links, buttons, theme toggles, search inputs) are reachable and operable via keyboard alone (`Tab`, `Shift + Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`).
- **Semantic HTML5 Structure**: Landmark elements (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`) and clear header hierarchy (`<h1>` through `<h3>`) to support assistive technology and screen readers.
- **Readable Typography**: Scalable rem/em sizing with legible line-heights and clear contrast ratios across background and foreground text.
- **Responsive Layout**: Fluid design accommodating zoom levels up to 200% without loss of content or functionality on desktop, tablet, and mobile displays.
- **Code Block Readability**: Clear color-coding and monospaced typography with distinct visual separation for commands and expected outputs.

---

## Supported Environments

The study guide is designed to be compatible with:

- Modern desktop browsers: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge.
- Mobile browsers: Chrome for Android, Safari for iOS.
- Screen readers: NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver (macOS / iOS), and TalkBack (Android).

---

## Known Limitations & Roadmap

While we strive for comprehensive accessibility, some areas are actively being enhanced:

1. **Terminal Diagrams & Flowcharts**: Text-based ASCII diagrams and illustrations are being supplemented with alternative text descriptions and text transcripts.
2. **Interactive Code Snippets**: Work is underway to provide dedicated "Copy to clipboard" keyboard shortcuts with screen-reader audio feedback.

---

## Feedback & How to Report Accessibility Barriers

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the RHCSA Study Guide. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please let us know:

- **GitHub Issues**: [Open an Accessibility Issue](https://github.com/Jani-shiv/rhcsa-study-guide/issues/new?template=accessibility.yml)
- **YouTube Community**: Connect with us on [DevOps ni Diary](https://youtube.com/@DevOpsniDiary)

When reporting an issue, please try to include:
- The webpage URL or chapter where the barrier occurred.
- The assistive technology (and version) you are using (e.g. NVDA, VoiceOver).
- Your web browser and operating system.
- A brief description of the problem and what you expected to experience.

We aim to respond to accessibility reports within 2 business days.
